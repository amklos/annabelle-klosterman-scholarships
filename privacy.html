Your information matters

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Annabelle Klosterman (“I,” “me,” or “this website”) handles information you provide when you visit annabelleklosterman.com or submit an inquiry through the website.

Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, I may collect your name, email address, whether you are a student, parent/guardian, educator, or other visitor, the student’s grade or education stage, the type of support you are interested in, and the message you send.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, student identification numbers, financial account information, passwords, or detailed education or health records through the form.

How information is used

I use inquiry information to respond to your message, answer questions about scholarship guidance, understand what kind of support may be relevant, and communicate about a potential service or next step. I do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

The website contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit the form, the information you enter is sent to Formspree so it can be delivered to me. Formspree may process information according to its own privacy practices and terms. You can review its privacy policy.

Cookies, analytics, and website hosting

This website does not currently ask you to create an account or make a purchase, and its page code does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process basic technical information, such as browser requests and IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

When information may be shared

I may rely on service providers, including Formspree and the website host, to operate the website and receive messages. I do not sell or rent inquiry information. I may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of the website or others.

Children and parent/guardian involvement

The website is intended for students and families. If a student is under 13, a parent or legal guardian should submit the contact form on the student’s behalf. Please do not submit information about a child under 13 without a parent or guardian’s involvement.

How long information is kept

I keep inquiry messages for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant communication, and manage business records. Formspree may retain submitted information under its own retention practices.

Your choices and questions

If you would like to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, use the contact form and explain your request. I may need to retain some information where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or services change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the current version was published.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the website contact form.

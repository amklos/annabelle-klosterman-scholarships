Questions are welcome

Let’s be real about scholarships.

It’s okay to ask how this works before you decide if it’s right for your family.

My promiseUseful advice, honest expectations, and the student’s own voice.
Do I need good grades?

No. I definitely wasn’t a 4.0 student, and plenty of scholarships aren’t based on merit or grades. We can look for opportunities that fit a student’s interests, experiences, and goals.

Can you help with STEM or cybersecurity scholarships?

Yes. I earned awards in those areas and can share what I learned about finding and applying for them.

Will you write my student’s essays?

No. Their own experiences and voice should come through. I can help them shape and revise their writing so it answers the prompt clearly.

Can I join as a parent?

Absolutely. Parents are welcome to be part of the conversation and the plan.

Can you guarantee a scholarship?

No one can promise a particular award. I can help students search thoughtfully, prepare stronger applications, and stay organized.

A little more about Annabelle
The story behind my approach

I know the scholarship search from the student side.

I was homeschooled, so I had to figure out the process without a traditional school counselor. I wasn’t valedictorian and I didn’t have perfect test scores. I started researching, keeping track of deadlines, and learning how to make my applications stronger.

I applied to more than 50 scholarships and earned over $525,000. I was offered three full rides. My scholarships were worth more than my tuition. After the university paid my bill, it sent me the extra funds in checks. With the stipends too, I was paid to go to college. I later built a career in cybersecurity, where I saw how much STEM scholarships can open doors.

Now I help students and families sort through the noise, focus on opportunities that fit, and make a plan they can actually follow. I won’t promise a particular award or write a student’s essay for them. I will give honest feedback and help them show their own strengths.
